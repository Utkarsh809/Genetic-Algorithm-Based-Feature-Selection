System architecture

Genetic Algorithm Feature Selection

A wrapper-style selector that evolves binary feature masks, scores each mask with cross-validation on the training split only, and is benchmarked on a held-out test split against three standard selectors. Every number on this page comes from the run in outputs/.

End-to-end pipeline

Training data flows through the GA and the baselines. Test data is touched once, at evaluation.

fitness = cv_accuracy − α · (k / n) = 1.0000 − 0.1 · (14 / 53) = 1.0000 − 0.0264 = 0.9736 ← best_fitness_ at generation 21

The penalty term is what separates a 14-feature mask from a 53-feature mask that scores the same in cross-validation. At α = 0.1, dropping one feature of 53 is worth about 0.0019 accuracy.

Inside the GA loop

The operators in GeneticFeatureSelector, shown on an 8-feature toy chromosome so every bit is visible. This toy is illustrative, not taken from the run.

parent A10011000fitness 0.947 (won tournament)

swap mask10101001random, p = 0.5 per bit; 1 takes the bit from A in child 1
child 111011010where(swap, A, B)
child 200010000where(swap, B, A)
mutated 200110000bit 2 flipped; if a child ends up all zeros, one random feature is switched on
ChromosomeA boolean vector of length n. Bit i is 1 when feature i is kept. The Wine run uses 53 bits.
Tournament selectionDraw 3 random individuals, keep the fittest. Example: draws with fitness 0.912, 0.947 and 0.931 return the 0.947 individual.
Mutation rateEach bit flips with probability 2 / n, about two flips per child. At n = 53 that is p ≈ 0.038.
ElitismThe two best masks pass unchanged, so best fitness never drops from one generation to the next.

Convergence on the traced run

Best fitness climbs from 0.914 to 0.974 while the population mean rises from 0.672 to 0.942 as random masks are replaced.

The gap between best CV accuracy and best fitness is the size penalty. At generation 21 it is 0.0264, which is 14 of 53 features at α = 0.1.

On this single split the three filters beat the GA by two test samples out of 54, and the GA kept eight noise columns against 2, 3 and 1. Across all 27 GA runs (3 datasets × 3 classifiers × 3 seeds) the mean test accuracy is 0.934 for the GA, 0.939 for MI, 0.936 for RFE, 0.940 for RF importance and 0.884 for all features. The GA trails the baselines by 0.2 to 0.6 points and beats using every column by 5 points while keeping 71 % fewer columns (20.9 against 72.3 on average). Fitting the mask to one classifier did not translate into higher test accuracy on these benchmarks.

Components

Two files, one reusable class and one experiment driver.

FileSymbolResponsibility
ga_feature_selection.pyGeneticFeatureSelectorscikit-learn compatible selector with fit, transform, get_support. Holds the population, the fitness cache and history_.
ga_feature_selection.py_score, _repairCached cross-validated fitness. Repair switches on random features when a mask has fewer than min_features.
run_experiment.pyload_datasetsBuilds the three benchmarks and appends Gaussian noise columns.
run_experiment.pybaseline_masksFits MI, RFE and RF importance on train data with k taken from the GA.
run_experiment.pyevaluate, summarizeRefits each classifier on the selected columns, scores test accuracy and macro-F1, aggregates over seeds.
run_experiment.pyplot_*Writes the accuracy, convergence and feature-reduction figures.

Design decisions

No test leakageThe GA, the CV folds and every baseline see only X_train. The test split is used once, for scoring.
Equal feature budgetBaselines take k from the GA result, so a difference in accuracy is not a difference in subset size.
Fitness cacheMasks are keyed by their bytes. Elites and repeated children cost nothing, which saved 19 of 618 evaluations in the traced run.
Penalty tuningAt α = 0.02 the GA kept 28 features on Wine with KNN in an earlier tuning run. At α = 0.1 the same setup kept 17.
Classifier swapRandom forest was replaced by logistic regression because a forest took about 60 s per GA run, against 15 to 66 s for the three final classifiers.
CostAbout 655 evaluations × 5 folds, roughly 3,300 model fits and 29 s per GA run on average.
